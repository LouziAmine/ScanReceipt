# Privacy policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026.

ScanReceipt is an open-source app that scans receipts. It is built to keep your data on your phone.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** The ScanReceipt project has no server, no account system, no analytics and no advertising. We cannot see
your receipts, your photos or how you use the app.

## What stays on your phone

| Data | Where it is stored |
| --- | --- |
| Receipt photos | The app's private storage |
| Receipt details (store, date, amounts, category, note, recognized text) | A database in the app's private storage |
| Settings and categories | The app's private storage |

Text recognition runs **on your phone**. Deleting a receipt removes its photo and data. Uninstalling the app removes
everything.

## Third-party components

On-device text recognition and, on Android, document scanning use **Google ML Kit**. Your receipt photos and their text
are processed on the device and are not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google technical information about its own
performance and use, and may download model updates. This does not include your receipts. See
[Google's ML Kit terms](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).

On iOS, document scanning uses Apple's VisionKit, which runs on the device.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Camera | Scan receipts |
| Photos | Import a receipt from your photo library |
| Notifications | Optional monthly backup reminder, scheduled on the phone |

## When data leaves your phone

Only when **you** decide: when you export, share or back up receipts, the file goes to the app or service you pick in
the system share sheet (email, cloud drive, messaging…). That service's own privacy policy then applies.

## Children

The app does not collect data from anyone, including children.
