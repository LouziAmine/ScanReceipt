Scan a receipt in seconds, with no account and no internet. Your data stays on your phone.

ScanReceipt reads your paper receipts for you: point the camera, and the store, the date, the total and the tax are filled in. Doubtful fields are highlighted, so you only check what needs checking.

FAST
• Scanner with edge detection and perspective correction
• On-device text recognition: works in airplane mode
• Categories suggested from the store name

USEFUL FOR TAXES AND EXPENSE REPORTS
• Totals per day, week, month and year, and per category
• Export to CSV (Excel), PDF with photos, or a ZIP of the photos
• Share a receipt as a photo or as text

PRIVATE
• No account, no server, no ads, no tracking
• Nothing leaves your phone unless you share it
• Open source (Apache 2.0)

NO DATA LOSS
• Full backup in a single file, with a monthly reminder
• Restore everything on a new phone

ANY CURRENCY, 7 LANGUAGES
• Reads the currency on the receipt and converts it at the rate of the purchase day
• English, French, Arabic, Chinese, Spanish, German and Portuguese
